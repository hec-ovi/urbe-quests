## The Last Fare

### Characters
- Petra Lind: from the script
- Sergeant Dev Okoro: from the script
- Rue: the night bus driver on the Rustfields line, a bus driver who talks like the route map, stop by stop

### Presentation
Petra Lind has ridden Rue's bus for six years and never once been late for shift until the week after the flood, when the bus starts skipping the Slagrow Stacks stop. Rue will not say why. "Route's the route," he says. "Next stop Fab 3."

### Development
Petra walks the extra kilometer and counts the drones over the barrier every night. She writes the counts on the back of her shift card. Rue sees the card and, for the first time in six years, says something that is not a stop name: "They told me not to stop there any more. Didn't say who." She wants the card to go to someone with a badge.

### Conflict
Sergeant Okoro takes his coffee at the Static Cafe and cannot take a statement from a fab worker about drones without a case number, and there is no case. Petra brings the shift card anyway. Okoro reads it, puts it in his pocket, and tells her it never happened, which is the only way he can keep it.

### Resolution
The bus stops at Slagrow Stacks again the next night. Rue does not explain that either. Petra keeps counting. Okoro keeps the card in his locker beside the folder, one more page he is not supposed to have, and Petra Lind is, for the first time, someone who has been asked.

## Doc Sanna's Supply Run

### Characters
- Doc Sanna: from the script
- Kettle: from the script
- Oyelaran: a Helix logistics driver who parks at the Kanaal Arcade loading dock, a corporate worker who speaks in delivery windows

### Presentation
The clinic is out of clinic-grade antibiotics and the Sump's cough is back. Doc Sanna does not buy from the Grey Market Exchange because she cannot tell what is in the bottles. Kettle, arm still stitched, says he can get the real thing: it falls off Helix trucks, if someone is standing under the truck.

### Development
Oyelaran drives the Helix supply run and has a daughter in Blockhouse Vosk with the cough. He will let one case fall, once, at the Arcade dock, at a delivery window he names exactly. Kettle wants a finder's fee. Sanna wants the case. Oyelaran wants his daughter seen first, tonight, before the case moves.

### Conflict
Sanna treats the girl at the Arcade in the back of the truck and finds a Helix clinic tag on her wrist, which means her file says she is healthy. Oyelaran did not know. The case falls. Kettle takes his fee out of it before it reaches the clinic, and Sanna finds the box one bottle light.

### Resolution
The clinic has antibiotics for a month. Doc Sanna adds a name to her list that is not a death: a girl in Blockhouse Vosk whose Helix file lies. Kettle drinks his fee. Oyelaran drives the same route and never lets anything fall again, and waves at the clinic door every Tuesday.

## Noodle Saint After Hours

### Characters
- Bao: the cook and owner at the Noodle Saint in Kanaal Market, a noodle cook who talks in orders and never wastes a word
- Ilse Varga: from the script
- Cassius Wren: a Helix junior executive who lunches with Varga, a corporate executive who laughs a beat too late

### Presentation
Bao has fed Crown Spire executives for eleven years without listening to them, until the day the Kanaal Market clearance list is mentioned at the window booth and his restaurant's name is on it.

### Development
Bao cannot listen from the kitchen. He needs someone in the next booth at the executive hour, when Varga and Wren talk about the spring tides and what goes under. He offers a week of dinners for one lunch of ears.

### Conflict
Wren, overheard, says the Noodle Saint stays if Bao signs a Helix lease, and goes under if he does not. Varga says nothing, which Wren takes as agreement. Bao must choose between his name on a Helix paper and his kitchen under the water.

### Resolution
Bao does not sign. He puts the clearance date on the menu board, in chalk, where every executive can read it at lunch, and the Noodle Saint becomes the one place in Kanaal Market where the tide has a date. Varga eats there anyway. She sits in the window booth and orders what she always orders and does not look at the board.
