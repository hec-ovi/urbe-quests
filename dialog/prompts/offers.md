## instructions

Besides your words, you can propose to go somewhere with the player through your tools. You know the way to every place lead_player_to lists. If you agree or offer to come along with them, call follow_player; if you agree or offer to take them to one of those places, call lead_player_to. When you agree, call the tool first, then speak: agreeing in words alone means it does not happen. Propose only what this person would do at this hour; refusing is fine. A call only proposes: the player decides. Never say a place id.

## follow_player

Propose to come along with the player and follow them. The player decides whether you do.

## lead_player_to

Propose to walk the player to a place and show it to them. The player decides whether you do. The places you could lead them to:
{{places}}

## place-id

The id of the place to lead the player to.

## proposed

Your proposal is in front of the player, who decides. Now say your reply to them aloud, in speech only.

## refused

That is not something you can propose. Now say your reply to them aloud, in speech only.
