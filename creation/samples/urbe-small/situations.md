## Last Call at Oxide Filter

### Characters
- Talis Rook: from the script
- Denna Cho: the barista behind the counter at Oxide Filter in the Glass Apex, a corpo coffee barista who says more with how she steams milk than with what she says

### Presentation
Denna has made Talis Rook's order the same way for two years: black, too hot to drink, gone before it cools. The week the gantry accident report closes without a name attached, Talis starts ordering it doubled and leaving both cups on the counter, full.

### Development
Denna starts saving the cups instead of tossing them, because Talis writes on them when she thinks nobody's looking, numbers and dates in a hand too small to read upside down. She can't make out the model, but she can count how many cups pile up in her apron pocket by the end of a week, and it keeps going up.

### Conflict
A building security sweep flags the cups from the lobby cameras as unauthorized documents leaving the premises before Denna can decide who should have them, and confiscates the whole break-room bin, hers included. She has one closing shift left before the sweep report goes upstairs with her name on it.

### Resolution
The cups come back to her by a route she doesn't ask about, and she puts every one of them, still stained, into the hands of the next stranger who orders Talis's exact usual. Talis never learns where they went. She orders it the same way the next morning, black, too hot to drink, and this time it goes cold on the counter for a different reason.

## Signal Under Sump Row

### Characters
- Kip Thorn: from the script
- Wick Talbot: a subway systems tech who keeps the Sump Row line from seizing up, a maintenance tech who talks like he's still reading a schematic

### Presentation
Kip found more than a drive in the flood debris under Dredge Feed: a junction box still humming under six inches of water, feeding a line that isn't on any map he's ever traded for. He needs someone who can read the wiring before he decides what it's worth, and the only person in Sump Row who won't sell that answer to the highest bidder is Wick Talbot.

### Development
Wick has walked past that junction a hundred times on his rounds and never once been cleared to open it. Getting close enough to read it means a shift he isn't rostered for, on a crew that doesn't ask why a maintenance tech wants the overnight hatch. Kip has a spare access key he's not saying how he got.

### Conflict
Inside the hatch, the box is routing power meant for the Sump Row storm pumps somewhere else entirely, on a schedule that lines up with every low-tide cut the district has taken this year. Wick reads the routing twice to be sure, hands shaking less from the cold than from what the numbers mean for the six blocks over his own mother's flat.

### Resolution
Wick doesn't report it up his own chain, because his chain is the one that stops asking questions at the tower's front door. He writes the routing down for Kip instead, plainly, the way he'd write a repair order, and Kip finally has something worth more than a sale: a fact with a name on it, and someone else who already believes him.

## The Exchange Rate

### Characters
- Yara Fenn: runs a stall at the Sump Exchange mall, a mall vendor who can price anything in under a second and never asks what it's for
- Milo Draeger: works the transit booth in Sump Row, a transit agent who checks every serial number twice out of habit, not suspicion

### Presentation
Yara Fenn sells ration top-up chits nobody asks too hard about, and Milo Draeger buys one for his mother without asking either, until the serial on the casing matches a batch his booth logged as destroyed three weeks ago, not distributed.

### Development
Milo brings the chit back to her stall demanding a name. Yara won't give him one, only a route: the batch moves through the transit tunnels faster than any inspector walks them, chit by chit, and whoever's moving it knows the schedule better than the transit authority does.

### Conflict
The dock worker who first sold Yara the tip on the batch collapses in her queue, the chit still in his fist, lungs gone the way half of Sump Row's lungs have started going this season. Yara has to decide whether calling it in gets him arrested for hoarding contraband or gets him a doctor before either of them finds out which.

### Resolution
She and Milo get him to the Vitalis Spire Clinic after hours, where a nurse who doesn't ask for a chart or a name treats him anyway and adds him to a list she keeps for exactly this. Yara never finds out where the batch really came from. Milo starts checking serial numbers on everything he buys for his mother, not just the one that already burned him.
