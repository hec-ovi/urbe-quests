## The Tuesday Barrel

### Characters
- Dessa Vey: from the script

### Presentation
Tuesday noon at the Sluice Grub stall, and a dock hand sits on the wet pavement beside the counter with his back to the barrel, grey in the face, a stranger kneeling over him and holding his wrist. The cup he drank from lies in the gutter. It did not come from Dessa's barrel; it came from a cheaper one two stalls down.

### Development
Dessa keeps ladling, because the queue does not stop for one man. "Mine's clean," she says to anyone who looks at her. "Two a cup. His was one, and you get what one buys."

### Conflict
When the player asks where clean water comes from on half rations, Dessa's smile goes thin. Telling means the barrel stops; not telling means the next cheap cup is somebody's child. "You want me to name the hand that feeds this street? Then you feed it."

### Resolution
She tells the player the barrel comes stamped from Weir Exchange every Tuesday, and she tells it quietly, the way people in Sump Row say things they cannot take back. The dock hand gets up on his own. Dessa pours him a cup of hers and does not charge.

## Tolerance

### Characters
- Ivo Lann: from the script

### Presentation
Ivo Lann has read a courier's figures on his slate and cannot stop reading them. In the lobby of Vantablack Dynamics he stops the player with a coffee he forgot to drink. "Hypothetically. If someone wanted to know whether a model was wrong or a counter was, they would look at the counter."

### Development
He will not go himself. A man from the tower at a Sump Row counter is a story, and he has spent his career making sure there are none. "I don't need to know. I need to know that I don't need to know."

### Conflict
The truth either clears his model or hangs a clerk on it, and both put his name in a report he does not want to be in.

### Resolution
The player stands at the Weir Exchange counter and sees the stamped measure go out short. Ivo gets his answer: the model is correct. He finds he does not feel any cleaner for it.

## Rust Bucket Credit

### Characters
- Tamsin Roe: a cook at the Rust Bucket Eatery, sharp, keeps her sentences short because the kitchen is loud.
- Hale Mercer: from the script

### Presentation
The Rust Bucket Eatery pays for its kitchen water twice: once in ration chits at Weir Exchange, and again by the cup at the end of the week when the ration runs dry. Tamsin Roe keeps both receipts in a tin behind the stove.

### Development
"Somebody's selling us our own water," Tamsin tells the player over the pass. "Mercer eats here. Mercer used to walk this street. Tell him what it costs."

### Conflict
Hale Mercer at Apex Oversight does not want another Sump Row story without paper. Tamsin will not come up the staircase herself; a cook seen at the precinct loses her suppliers.

### Resolution
The player carries Tamsin's price to Hale. He listens, writes the number down and says he will eat somewhere else for a month, which on that street means he has started watching.
