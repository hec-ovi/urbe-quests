# Step catalog

Nine step kinds. Each works in any era; what changes is the scenario you dress it in. Pick what fits this world, skip what does not (no hacking jobs in a medieval town, no camel drives in a corporate arcology).

## goto (reach a place)
- Cyberpunk: ride the elevated line to a flooded dock warehouse.
- Medieval: walk the pilgrim road to the shrine outside the wall.
- Modern: show up at the parking garage on level 4.

## observe (watch a district)
- Cyberpunk: watch drone traffic over the industrial fields until the pattern breaks.
- Medieval: study the market square to learn which stalls pay the watch.
- Ancient: survey the temple quarter at dusk for the procession route.

## talk (find a person and speak)
- Cyberpunk: catch the clinic medic between patients; she only talks at work.
- Medieval: the tavern keeper speaks freely only behind the bar.
- Any era: a worker who will not talk at home, only on shift.

## listen (sit near two people talking)
- Cyberpunk: take the next booth while two executives argue over lunch.
- Medieval: nurse an ale within earshot of the steward and the smith.
- Modern: ride the same train car as two colleagues who talk too loudly.

## pickup (take an item)
- Cyberpunk: recover a data chip stashed under a market stall.
- Medieval: collect a sealed letter left inside a chapel bench.
- Ancient: retrieve an amphora seal from the harbor master's post.

## deliver (bring something to a place)
- Cyberpunk: drive an unregistered car to a garage before dawn shift.
- Desert era: lead a camel loaded with cloth to the caravan district.
- Aviation era: fly a crop plane one district over and land it quietly.

## steal (take what is guarded)
- Cyberpunk: lift a keycard from a security desk during rounds.
- Medieval: take the guild ledger from the counting room.
- Modern: pocket a prototype from a trade show floor.

## assassinate (end someone)
- Cyberpunk: a fixer wants an executive to miss every future meeting.
- Medieval: the steward must not survive the harvest feast.
- Use rarely; it is permanent, and the dead give no quests.

## work (take a job to gain access)
- Cyberpunk: sign on as night janitor to reach the restricted floors.
- Medieval: hire on as a tannery hand to enter the yard unseen.
- Modern: temp at the front desk to learn the visitor log.
