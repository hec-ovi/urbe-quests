# Artifact catalog

Seven item kinds. An artifact is introduced with whose it is and what it means to them, never only what it is. Physical kinds start placed at a parcel (then a pickup step) or in a person's hands (then a steal step, or a talk step that gives it). Information is never picked up: a person tells it (a talk, listen or observe step gives it) and a later step may need it.

## device
- Cyberpunk: a data chip with the only copy of a boardroom recording, stashed by the barista who made it.
- Modern: a burner phone with one saved number, left by a witness who no longer answers it.

## weapon
- Cyberpunk: a printed pistol with the fixer's serial ground off; whoever holds it holds her.
- Medieval: a hunting knife with a family crest that should not be in this city.

## document
- Cyberpunk: an internal memo naming the tenements to be cleared; the clerk who copied it wants it out of her hands.
- Medieval: a sealed letter, a confession, left where only one person would look.
- Modern: a shipping manifest with one line struck out.

## key
- Cyberpunk: a security keycard for the restricted floors, tied to the desk officer's name.
- Medieval: the iron key to the counting room, on the steward's belt.
- Modern: a hotel room card that opens a room that was never booked.

## substance
- Cyberpunk: a case of clinic-grade medicine that only falls off corpo trucks.
- Medieval: a vial from the apothecary that cures or kills depending on the dose.

## valuable
- Cyberpunk: a bearer credit chit worth a year in the tenements.
- Medieval: a reliquary the chapel has already reported stolen.
- Ancient: an amphora seal that proves a shipment was paid.

## information
- Cyberpunk: the name of the officer the tower pays, told by the medic at work.
- Medieval: which stalls pay the watch, learned by watching the square.
- Any era: what two people said when they thought nobody was listening.
