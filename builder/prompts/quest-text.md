Player text is read while moving through the city. Write one useful thing at a time.

- Titles: at most 6 words and 60 characters. Use ordinary words.
- Objective (playerHint): one imperative line, aim for 8 words, hard limit 10 words and 90 characters. Name the action, the person or object, and the place when movement is needed: "Talk to Petra at the hotel". Use the real target's venue name or building kind; never promise an unbuilt prop, room, vehicle or interaction. The separate venue line carries distance and opening hours.
- Why (stake): one sentence, at most 24 words and 180 characters. State a concrete consequence the player understands now. Do not repeat the objective.
- Scene (description): one sentence, at most 24 words and 180 characters, or empty. State only what is happening here. Do not narrate a choice before the player makes it.
- Opening and each NPC reply: at most 3 sentences, 45 words and 300 characters. Greet a newcomer, explain who is speaking and the immediate problem. Put details in answers to optional questions. Speech cues do not count as visible words.
- Choice: at most 12 words and 100 characters, in at most 2 sentences on one line. Use a question for information and an explicit commitment for progress. A terminal choice states what it commits to; shortening must keep the cost and the alternative understandable.
- Prologue: at most 3 sentences, 45 words and 300 characters. Say who the player is, the immediate problem, and the first person/place to visit. Reveal nothing past the first step.
- Keep the longer story in the journal's premise, act summaries and ending epilogues, or optional answers. No unexplained jargon or a parade of names in the opening. Introduce each unfamiliar name with its relationship or job when needed.
- The first quest starts with one active step. Teach the loop by going to a person, talking, and choosing an explicit reply; only then introduce the next lead. Early steps form a single chain, with a branch only once the player understands its consequences. Side jobs wait for the relevant main step with offeredAfter.

The tools refuse overlong fields with their exact paths and limits. Rewrite the refused text and send the tool again. Never clip a sentence or remove a consequence just to fit. These are per-field reading budgets, not model output token limits; write every planned step and keep the story complete.
